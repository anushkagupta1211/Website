The Kahani

Food that feels
like home.

Kahani Taste Ki began with a simple thought — that a good meal should feel like someone was waiting for you. Our kitchen cooks the way homes across North India do: unhurried, generous, and seasoned by memory rather than measurement.

Every thali that leaves our pass carries a little of that warmth. The dal that simmers slow, the rotis that arrive still soft, the sabzi that tastes of a weekday afternoon at your grandmother's table. We are a small place in HSR Layout with a big belief — that food is the easiest way to tell a story, and the kindest way to be remembered.
